######## note (added after the run) ########
First run, kept as the finding. The export side was already complete
(coverage 100 %), the erasure failed with HTTP 500 before touching any row:
Hibernate refused to flush managed Link entities whose source, the person,
had just been removed (TransientPropertyValueException). The schema's
ON DELETE CASCADE never got a chance because the ORM rejected the unit of
work first. The transaction rolled back, the subject stayed intact, no
partial erasure occurred. Fix in PersonPrivacyService.erase: remove the
links explicitly before the object. Re-measured in the next report file.
